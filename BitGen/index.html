Terms of Service, Privacy Policy & Legal Compliance

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 • IT Act 2000 • GIGW 3.0

1. Terms of Service & Statutory Citizen Entitlement

This National Digital Interoperability Gateway (NDIG) is an official electronic public services delivery platform operated by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD), Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India. Use of this portal is governed under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Any unauthorized automated scraping, denial-of-service, or fraudulent application submission is strictly prohibited and subject to criminal prosecution under Section 66 of the IT Act.

2. Privacy Policy & Citizen Consent Framework

In strict compliance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, data exchange across participating ministries (UIDAI, MoRTH, CRS, Revenue, Agriculture, MCA) is triggered solely upon explicit, revocable, and purpose-bound citizen consent. No biometric data is stored by the Gateway. Interoperability queries utilize cryptographic SHA-256 tokens and zero-knowledge proofs to verify credentials without retaining raw departmental data.

3. Legal Validity of Electronically Issued Certificates

All digital certificates issued via this gateway (including Caste, Income, Domicile, Smart DL, Birth, and Company Registrations) are authenticated with Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) issued by CCA-licensed Certifying Authorities. Under Section 4 and Section 5 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, these electronically signed Gazette documents hold full statutory equivalence to physical paper certificates with physical seals.

4. Statutory Timelines & Public Services Guarantee

Public services provided through this platform are notified under statutory citizen service delivery legislation. Every citizen has a statutory entitlement to receive services within the stipulated Citizen Charter timeframes. Failure to adhere to specified timelines without valid administrative reasons attracts mandatory statutory penalty on the designated competent authority.

5. Hyperlinking & Content Copyright Policy

Material published on this portal may be reproduced without prior formal permission for educational and administrative verification purposes, provided proper attribution to the Ministry of Electronics & IT is explicitly stated. Links to external portals are provided solely for user convenience.
